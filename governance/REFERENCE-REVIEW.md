# Constitution reference review

Reviewed 7 September 2026. Supporting explanation, not part of the constitution.

The revised draft remains for a nonprofit unincorporated association in England. Its name is still blank; Pennine is not selected. The Pit Combat Academy remains its public programme. This review does not adopt the document or change the chosen legal structure.

## Supplied example

Read the complete text of `THE CYCLING IMAAM CONSTITUTION OFFICIAL.docx`, supplied from Downloads, adopted 1 November 2021. It is an example of another group's arrangements, not evidence of legal approval for this Association. No personal names or signatures have been transferred.

| Reference provision | Treatment in this draft |
|---|---|
| Separate aims, practical objectives and powers | Added practical coaching, volunteering and outreach objectives in 2.4; made the staffing power explicit in 3.1. No cycling programme or clinical-health claim imported. |
| Defined chair, secretary and treasurer duties | Added 7.5, with accountability and delegated-authority limits. |
| Committee can make additional rules | Added 7.6, expressly preventing operational rules from changing constitutional membership or election rights. |
| Membership register and fair hearing | Retained the existing register, reasons, supporter and impartial appeal provisions. Clarified that membership continues until it ends under 4.4, instead of importing three-year expiry. |
| Annual election, reports and accounts | Retained the existing AGM provisions and adult member elections; all elected committee members now have signature spaces at adoption. |
| Committee receives expenses only | Not imported: it would contradict the agreed ability to pay a founder for actual coaching. Clause 8 keeps office voluntary while requiring independent approval, a separate written agreement and an amount or maximum amount for actual services. |
| Committee size and chair's extra vote | Not imported. The example's management section permits three officers plus up to two others, while its AGM section says three to eight. This draft consistently uses five to seven; tied decisions fail. |
| Organisational membership and a fixed ten-person AGM quorum | Not imported. This draft uses individual membership, adult voting and its existing scalable quorum. |
| Two-thirds dissolution and Oldham-only recipient | Retained this draft's three-quarters dissolution vote and similar-purpose nonprofit recipient with asset restrictions; its area also includes Rochdale and Greater Manchester. |

## Action Together and official checks

[Action Together's starting-a-group page](https://www.actiontogether.org.uk/starting-group) supports identifying beneficiaries, purposes and operating area before selecting the structure. It outlines officer roles, governing-document content, financial controls and risk management. It asks for two unrelated bank signatories: clause 9.2 now expressly requires unrelated dual approvers. Its three-person starting point is general guidance; this draft's five-person committee and three independent approvers are deliberate governance choices, not universal legal requirements. The page explicitly does not offer downloadable templates. No request has been sent to its Development Team.

The linked reference route used was the [Charity Commission governing-document guide](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-write-your-charitys-governing-document-cc22b) and its [official model document collection](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/setting-up-a-charity-model-governing-documents), rather than treating a CIO association model as interchangeable with an unincorporated association.

- The [small-charity model GD4](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a80a1d440f0b62302694a00/GD4.pdf) says it is for charities below £5,000 annual income that do not own a building or employ people and do not intend to register. Its payment prohibition and stated scope make it unsuitable for wholesale adoption for the proposed academy.
- The [unincorporated charitable-association model GD3](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5cf667e440f0b60a31e0aaf8/GD3.pdf) was reviewed for governance coverage, member elections, controlled payments and asset restrictions. It is a charity model: adopting its label would not establish that the proposed purposes satisfy charity law. The bespoke draft retains its explicit status review and does not claim Commission approval.
- Current [goods/services payment guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/charities-paying-a-trustee-or-a-connected-person-understand-the-rules-cc11/paying-a-trustee-or-a-connected-person-for-providing-goods-or-services-to-the-charity--2) supports recording need, reasonable price and a separate written agreement before supply where charity rules apply. [Employment of a trustee or connected person](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/charities-paying-a-trustee-or-a-connected-person-understand-the-rules-cc11/employing-a-trustee-or-connected-person) has separate authority requirements. Recusal alone is insufficient; clause 8.5 preserves that distinction.
- [GOV.UK on unincorporated associations](https://www.gov.uk/unincorporated-associations) confirms this is not incorporation and does not remove personal responsibility for debts and commitments. The draft preserves its liability wording and member approval for major commitments. Charity registration and CASC remain separate status questions; CASC remains deferred.
