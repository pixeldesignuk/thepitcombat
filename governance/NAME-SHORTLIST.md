# Sport and development name shortlist

Revised 7 September 2026. **Suggestions only: no name selected, adopted, reserved or registered.** Replaces the rejected Pennine direction. The aim is an energetic, accessible name for fundraising and governance, centred on sport and personal development. The public academy brand remains **The Pit Combat Academy**.

## Five options

| Rank | Proposed organisation name | Fit | Tradeoff / preliminary finding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Next Chapter Sports Association** | Recommended: opportunity and progression for young people and adults, with a clear sports purpose. | Longer formal name; “Next Chapter” needs its sports descriptor. No exact organisation match identified in returned search material. |
| 2 | **Built Through Sport** | Short and memorable; expresses how the academy develops people through training. Strongest connection to The Pit's existing voice. | Reads like a mission or campaign; the phrase appears in ordinary sports writing, so distinctiveness needs assessment. |
| 3 | **Strong Start Sport and Development** | Active and reassuring for parents; connects participation with confidence and skills. | Suggests beginners and younger participants more than established adult competitors. No exact organisation match identified in returned search material. |
| 4 | **Earned Futures Sports** | Connects discipline today with opportunity tomorrow; fits the academy's firm, values-led tone. | “Earned” is demanding; supporting copy should communicate accessible participation. No exact organisation match identified in returned search material. |
| 5 | **Momentum Community Sports** | Communicates activity, collective progress and a broader sports purpose. | Most crowded option retained: UK Momentum Sports already provides athletics coaching, and Momentum Children's Charity fundraises. Lower priority for that reason. |

**Best fit: Next Chapter Sports Association.** **Strongest brand connection: Built Through Sport.** These are naming judgments, not availability findings or predictions of grant success.

## Existing-use findings

The initial suggestions were evaluated before forming this list:

- **Rise Sport & Community** was replaced because several existing organisations use Rise Sport, including an Australian operator with a community-focused sports offer. This is naming congestion, not a finding of a UK legal conflict. [RISE Sport](https://rise.com.au/)
- **Progress Sports Association** was replaced because Progress Sports has existing UK sports-training use. The subject-area overlap makes it a weaker choice. [Progress Sports case studies](https://progress-sports.co.uk/case-studies/)
- **Forward Sport & Development** was replaced after searches surfaced Forward Sports businesses and a UK Forward Sports Coaching company listing. That listing was not independently verified against a live official record; it is not treated as proof of a legal conflict.
- **Momentum Community Sports** remains a fifth-choice reserve despite nearby names: [Momentum Sports](https://momentumsports.co.uk/) provides UK athletics coaching; [Momentum Children's Charity](https://www.momentumcharity.org/get-involved/fundraise/) has an established fundraising identity.
- **Built Through Sport** appeared as descriptive wording, including in an NCAA programme heading. Descriptive use alone does not establish an organisation-name conflict. [NCAA programme](https://www.ncaa.org/what-we-do/community-engagement/special-olympics/)
