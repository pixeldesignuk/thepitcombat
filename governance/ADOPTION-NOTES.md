# Constitution adoption notes

Revised 7 September 2026 after reviewing the supplied Cycling Imaam constitution and Action Together guidance. Supporting notes only; not part of the constitution. The comparison and source links are in REFERENCE-REVIEW.md.

## What has been prepared

CONSTITUTION.md is the editable source. CONSTITUTION.html contains the same text in a self-contained printable layout. Both remain **draft, not adopted**. The organisation name and adoption record are intentionally blank. The Pit Combat Academy is the proposed working brand; it does not name a second legal body.

The proposed structure is an England nonprofit unincorporated association. Its broader community sport and youth-development purposes cover the current combat academy and possible related activities; they do not claim that additional programmes already exist. No registration, tax recognition, bank opening or legal adoption has occurred through preparing these documents.

## Before adoption

1. Select and check the association name using NAME-SHORTLIST.md. Insert the same final name in both document versions and all subsequent records.
2. Assess the purposes and intended operation to determine whether the association is charitable in law. A label saying “nonprofit” or “not registered” does not settle this. If the purposes are exclusively charitable for public benefit, charity duties can apply before registration. Obtain a review of the objects and payment provisions on that basis; use the Charity Commission model if choosing a CIO instead.
3. Identify at least five consenting adult founding members for the committee, including chair, secretary and treasurer as separate people, with at least three independent of paid coaches/suppliers and their families, households and business interests. Father and son cannot approve one another's coaching payments. Record conflicts before appointing suppliers or entering employment arrangements.
4. Confirm the 31 March year end, 30-day ordinary voting qualification, member election system and financial controls are workable. The five-person committee minimum and three independent approvers are drafting choices for credible governance, not assertions of a universal statutory requirement.
5. Hold a founding meeting, agree membership, elect the committee and approve the final constitution by a recorded resolution. Retain the signed attendee list, minutes and completed adoption page, with signatures from every elected committee member; give every member access to the adopted text. Founding members vote immediately.
6. Arrange an account in the adopted name with dual authorisation by unrelated people, approve the first budget, and adopt the operational policies. Record any paid coaching contract only after independent approval and the applicable employment/tax and, where relevant, charity-law requirements have been checked. Record an amount or maximum amount payable in the separate written agreement. This draft permits reasonable service payments subject to those controls; it does not provide blanket authority for charity trustee employment.

The constitution sets governance and powers, rather than a class timetable or price list. The first-session offer, current monthly prices and provisional training hours belong in operational records and can change through the committee's ordinary published fee-setting process. They are not conditions of adopting this constitution.

## Liability and status

An unincorporated association does not acquire separate legal personality through this constitution. People entering contracts can remain personally exposed; insurance does not replace incorporation or necessarily cover every obligation. Review the entity and signatory position before a premises lease, employment agreement or borrowing. The constitution expressly requires member approval for major commitments and does not compel an individual guarantee. [GOV.UK: unincorporated associations](https://www.gov.uk/unincorporated-associations)

Charitable status depends on purposes and public benefit. Registration duties and the right structure must be assessed separately; CIOs must register to exist, whereas the ordinary Charity Commission registration threshold is generally annual income of £5,000 for charities which are not exempt or excepted. A community association need not be registered merely to be formed, but that does not exempt it from tax, employment or charity duties that apply to its actual activities. [Setting up a charity](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-set-up-a-charity-cc21a), [charity structures](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/charity-types-how-to-choose-a-structure-cc22a)

**CASC is deferred, not assumed.** It is HMRC tax status, not incorporation, and therefore does not resolve personal liability. Current HMRC multi-sport guidance explicitly lists kickboxing as ineligible and requires all sports of a multi-sport club to be eligible. The earlier offer included kickboxing; the latest skeleton lists wrestling, MMA, Thai boxing and BJJ. Check each actual discipline against the current recognised-sport criteria rather than assuming Thai boxing and kickboxing have the same classification. Do not describe this constitution as CASC compliant or submit on the old assumption. CASC and charity registration are alternative routes; choosing CASC makes a later charity transition more complex. [HMRC detailed guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/community-amateur-sports-clubs-detailed-guidance-notes/community-amateur-sports-clubs-detailed-guidance-notes), [HMRC multi-sport eligibility](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/community-amateur-sports-clubs-detailed-guidance-notes/annex-3-multi-sports-clubs)

Fundraising eligibility is set by each funder and the actual organisational arrangements. The broader name does not itself confer eligibility. Do not describe the association as a registered charity or CASC or advertise Gift Aid without the appropriate basis.

## Drafting reference points

- [Action Together: starting a group](https://www.actiontogether.org.uk/starting-group). Useful local guidance on purposes, committee roles, structure, bank controls and risk. The page does not supply downloadable constitutions; it recommends discussing the appropriate document with its Development Team. No contact has been made on the Association's behalf.
- [Charity Commission: governing documents and model constitutions](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-write-your-charitys-governing-document-cc22b). This draft is a bespoke association document, not an approved Charity Commission model.
- [Charity Commission: payments to trustees and connected people](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/charities-paying-a-trustee-or-a-connected-person-understand-the-rules-cc11). If charity law applies, service supply and employment must be considered under their respective rules; recusal alone is not sufficient authority.

For printing, open CONSTITUTION.html locally and use the browser's Print / Save as PDF. Review the draft label and blank fields before treating any copy as adopted. If editing the source, update both versions together and verify that their wording matches.
