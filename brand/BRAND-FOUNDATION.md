# The Pit Combat Academy — Brand Foundation

**Date:** 2026-08-17 · **Stage:** discovery locked (01–04) · **Supersedes:** `BRAND-BRIEF.md` (archived, dual-audience/maktab direction dropped)

The venture is now **combat-academy only** — a UK multi-discipline martial-arts academy (Boxing · MMA · Muay Thai/Kickboxing · BJJ/Grappling) for **young people and adults**, run by a **father and son** (background story, not the headline). Greater Manchester — Oldham/Rochdale corridor.

---

## 01 · Audience

Two rings of one family:
- **Parents** who want a kid forged into someone with confidence, discipline, and a crew.
- **Serious amateurs / fighters** who want a real competitive room.

Same promise to both: **this is where you're made.** The pit is the crucible — you go in soft, come out hard.

**Competitive field (local):** Team Taz Muay Thai (owns the *charity/community* angle, DIY brand), Stealth BJJ (BJJ-led), Oldham Fight Academy (competitive MMA), Manchester Muay Thai / Fighters Academy (single-discipline). All single-discipline-led and template-branded.

**Three ownable gaps:**
1. **Multi-discipline completeness** — every art under one roof as one system.
2. **Development story** — the kid-to-competitor pipeline; nobody frames "we forge a fighter." (Team Taz owns *charity*; The Pit owns *development/legacy* — where the father-and-son thread lives.)
3. **Brand craft** — the whole field runs on template sites/generic visuals; a well-made identity is an unfair advantage.

## 02 · Voice — *The Hard Mentor, values-led*

Firm, few words, declarative. Leads with **respect · discipline · family · honour · being made.** Believes in you but won't coddle. A coach who pushes because he cares.

Signature lines:
- "We don't build members. We build people."
- "Discipline first. Everything else follows."
- "This is where you're made."

**Never:** hype/cheese, emoji-spam, threatening (parents must trust it), corporate, or apologetic-soft.
**Avoid words:** journey, unleash, beast-mode, warrior, crush-it.

## 03 · Visual — *Contemporary, sharp, type-forward*

Angular + high-contrast (modern MMA) meets bold monochrome lettering (streetwear fight-culture). **Palette: black + red.**

**Hard constraints:** mono-safe (stamps/embroidery/engraving); scales app-icon-tiny → building-signage-huge; works on dark and light — rashguard, mat, wall, web.

**Mark rule:** **abstract / geometric / typographic only — no people, no animals.** Toughness from weight, angle, letterform.

**Bans:** flames/tribal/dragons, neon/gamer glow, cartoon/kids-club brights, crossed-gloves/fist clichés, heritage-crest, quiet-minimal.

**Live concept directions** (from discovery):
1. **Wordmark-led** — bold combat-plate "THE PIT" with a red cut/notch; icon optional.
2. **Sharp abstract mark** — the pit/arena in plan or section, or a **P** built from that geometry.

*(Retired: heritage crest, nested-emblem, quiet-premium — ruled out at stage 03.)*

## 04 · Offer

**Sells:** kids/juniors classes · adult group classes · 1-to-1 / private + women's — across all four disciplines.

**Differentiator (the lead line):** **the kid-to-competitor pipeline — *we take a child and forge a fighter.***

Payments/enrolment: martial-arts platform + GoCardless (per `../MASTER-PLAN.md`, WS7). Logo lock is the **soft-launch trigger** (WS6).
