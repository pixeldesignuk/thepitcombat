The Pit · fresh start · hand-built comps

Four ideas, not four decorations

Round 1's AI batch was the genre's centre of mass — binned. These four are drawn by hand around actual concepts of a pit. They're direction sketches, not finished letterforms: pick the territory and the winner gets properly crafted.

D1 · Sunken

The word hangs below ground level — the line is the floor everyone else stands on; THE PIT lives underneath it. The red I is the only letter breaking the surface: the way in, and the way up.

D2 · Step Down

P · I · T each sit one step lower — the word itself descends. The red staircase is the pit's edge. (Flip it and it's the kid-to-competitor climb — same mark, both stories.)

D3 · The Slab

One solid plate — the mat, the block, the badge. Letters knocked out of the mass, red I burning through. Born as an app icon / patch / mat stamp; the wordmark version extends the same letterforms.

D4 · The Drop

Closest to your CLUB reference: chunky rounded logotype, angled THE tucked in, ® for legitimacy — but with one move that makes it ours: the I has fallen into the pit, dropped and red.
