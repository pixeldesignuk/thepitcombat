01 The mark

The descent

Two chevrons, pointed at the floor. Rank stripes turned downward — into the pit. Rank is earned, and so is this: you go down, you get worked, you come back harder. The doubling is depth — where you start, and where you're going.

  • The chevron points down. Always. Flipped up, it's somebody else's logo.
  • Two bars. Never one, never three.
  • Descent angle is 39.5°. Redraw nothing — scale the master vector.
  • The system constant is 0.16. Bar weight = 0.16 × mark width. Wordmark tracking = 0.16em. Keep that number.

02 Lockups

Four ways to sign

One identity, four configurations. Pick by the space, not by mood. When in doubt, use the primary.

Colourways

Full colour on Pit Black or Bone. Everything else is one colour — solid black or solid bone. Embroidery, engraving, stamps: always one colour.

  • Type is never red. Red belongs to the mark and to accents — not to letters in the logo.
  • "Combat Academy" is written, not drawn. The descriptor left the logo — where context needs it, set it as supporting type: Syne Bold, uppercase, tracked.
  • On photography: mono bone over a black scrim. Never full colour on a busy image.

03 Colour

Three colours. That's it.

Black is the ground. Bone does the talking. Red is the strike — the mark, a rule, one word. If red is everywhere, it's nowhere.

Working ratio. Red sits under ten percent of any surface — a strike, not a wash.

  • Red carries large elements only. It clears ~4.5:1 contrast on black and on bone — fine for the mark and display type, never for body text.
  • No new colours. No greys in the brand voice, no gradients, no glow. Functional UI greys live in product, not in identity.
  • Red never sits on red, black never on black.

04 Typography

Syne carries the weight

The wordmark is set in Syne ExtraBold — wide, geometric, planted. Display type uses the same voice. Body copy steps back into Archivo, a working grotesk that stays out of the way.

Display — Syne ExtraBold 800 · uppercase · tracking 0.16em Headlines, signage, merch

This is where you're made

Subhead — Syne Bold 700 · uppercase · tracking 0.08em Section titles, class names

Boxing · MMA · Muay Thai · BJJ

Body — Archivo 400 / 500 / 600 · sentence case Paragraphs, UI, schedules

We don't build members. We build people. Juniors train Monday to Thursday; the competitive room opens Friday. Discipline first — everything else follows.

  • Display is always uppercase, always tracked. 0.16em at wordmark scale, never tighter than 0.08em.
  • No substitutes on brand surfaces. In plain documents Arial does the job — the brand stays in the logo.
  • Both faces are free (Google Fonts). Syne + Archivo, variable weights, no licence cost.

05 Clearspace & size

Give it room

The unit is X — the weight of one chevron bar (0.16 × mark width). Keep at least 1X of empty space on every side of any lockup. Nothing enters that zone: no type, no photos, no other marks.

  • Embroidery and engraving: mark or wordmark only, one colour, ≥ 25mm wide.
  • Favicon and app icon: mark only, on Pit Black.

06 Misuse

Eight ways to ruin it

The files are final. Scale them, place them, leave them alone. Also banned by the brand foundation: flames, tribal, dragons, neon glow, crossed gloves, crests.

07 Voice

The hard mentor

Firm, few words, declarative. Believes in you and won't coddle you. Leads with respect, discipline, family, honour. Parents must trust it; fighters must rate it.

"We don't build members. We build people."

"Discipline first. Everything else follows."

"This is where you're made."

Sounds like

  • Short sentences. Full stops do the work.
  • Plain claims. "Four disciplines. One room." — not "a transformative martial arts experience".
  • The pipeline, always. We take a child and forge a fighter.
  • Respect in every line. Firm is not threatening.

Never

  • Hype or cheese. No exclamation marks doing push-ups.
  • Emoji spam. One, rarely. Better: none.
  • Corporate padding. No "solutions", no "offerings".
  • Soft apology. We state, we don't plead.
